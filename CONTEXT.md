# Centauri Cluster Journey Model

A shared vocabulary for describing the Centauri Cluster, its systems and Gates of Heaven, and the relativistic journeys made through it.

## Language

**Centauri Cluster**:
The star cluster containing the systems and Gates of Heaven represented by the model.

**System**:
A star system that occupies a position in the Centauri Cluster and may contain a hierarchy of route-relevant orbital bodies, barycenters, and multiple spatially separated Gates of Heaven.
_Avoid_: Star, destination

**Orbital Anchor**:
A star, planet, moon, or barycenter represented because it determines a Gate of Heaven’s orbit or has canonical relevance.
_Avoid_: Every celestial body, scenery

**Canonical System**:
A System whose existence or properties come from the novels or, where the novels are silent, other official EXODUS material.
_Avoid_: Real system, official system

**Generated System**:
A reproducibly generated System used to fill portions of the Centauri Cluster not described by canon. It remains visibly distinct from a Canonical System but may participate in journeys.
_Avoid_: Fake system, random star

**Provenance**:
The origin and authority of an individual property, distinguishing novel canon, supplementary official material, provisional assumptions, and generated content. An entity may therefore have a canonical identity and generated properties.
_Avoid_: Truth, confidence

**Canonical Claim**:
An exact value, range, or qualitative statement made by a cited canonical source about a property or Journey.
_Avoid_: Calculated value, ground truth

**Derived Result**:
A value or range calculated from model inputs and physical rules, kept distinct from any Canonical Claim it can be compared with.
_Avoid_: Canonical value, quoted duration

**Canon Discrepancy**:
A visible disagreement between a Canonical Claim and a Derived Result; neither is silently changed to match the other.
_Avoid_: Correction, override

**Scenario**:
A reproducible realization of the Centauri Cluster with a chosen population, procedural seed, generation rules, T+0 epoch, and reversible overrides of sourced or generated properties.
_Avoid_: Save, universe

**Gate of Heaven**:
An installation orbiting an Orbital Anchor that instantaneously accelerates a comoving ZPZ-capable ship to a fixed cruise speed of 0.999c or decelerates an arriving ship to match the Gate’s orbital velocity. Multiple Gates of Heaven within one System may be separated by days of sublight travel.
_Avoid_: Portal, wormhole, jump gate

**ZPZ Generator**:
Required ship equipment that freezes the ship’s atoms during a Gate of Heaven’s instantaneous acceleration or deceleration, preserving the ship and its occupants.
_Avoid_: Shield, drive

**Gate Connection**:
A bidirectional interstellar pairing of exactly two Gates of Heaven through which a ship can make an Interstellar Cruise. Each Gate of Heaven belongs to exactly one Gate Connection.
_Avoid_: Wormhole, teleport link

**Interstellar Cruise**:
Constant-speed travel at 0.999c in the Cluster Frame from a departure Gate of Heaven to the Arrival Intercept of its paired Gate.
_Avoid_: Jump, gate transit

**Ship Profile**:
The constant proper acceleration, braking, and maximum sublight-speed capabilities that determine an In-system Transfer.
_Avoid_: Ship class, gate speed

**In-system Transfer**:
Sublight powered flight between spatially separated Gates of Heaven in the same System, often required between consecutive interstellar legs. It begins and ends comoving with the respective Gates, and its elapsed times derive from a three-dimensional intercept and the Ship Profile.
_Avoid_: Dwell, gate connection

**Arrival Intercept**:
The future position of a destination Gate of Heaven at which an arriving ship completes its relativistic cruise.
_Avoid_: System center, static endpoint

**Dwell**:
Time a ship spends comoving with a Gate of Heaven rather than traveling. A Dwell may be user-chosen or inserted by the planner when waiting for better orbital geometry produces an earlier arrival, and Ship Proper Time follows the Gate’s worldline.
_Avoid_: Transfer, layover

**Journey**:
The continuous worldline of one ZPZ-capable ship traveling between selected Gates of Heaven along one or more Gate Connections, including required In-system Transfers and any Dwells. A Journey begins at a scenario-relative epoch and proceeds without gate-access delays.
_Avoid_: Jump, teleport

**Route Plan**:
A candidate path for a Journey. The preferred Route Plan has the earliest nominal arrival in Cluster Coordinate Time; its uncertainty bounds identify when another route might be faster, without penalizing generated connections.
_Avoid_: Itinerary, shortest path

**Journey Timeline**:
The ordered phases of a Journey, showing cumulative Cluster Coordinate Time, Ship Proper Time, and their aging difference at every event.
_Avoid_: Totals, animation

**Cluster Frame**:
The shared inertial reference frame in which the Centauri Cluster's Systems and Gates of Heaven are treated as stationary.
_Avoid_: Origin frame, Earth time

**Cluster Coordinate Time**:
The elapsed time between Journey events measured in the Cluster Frame.
_Avoid_: Origin time, outside time

**Ship Proper Time**:
The elapsed time experienced by a clock traveling with the ship during a Journey.
_Avoid_: Ship time, traveler time

**Aging Difference**:
Cluster Coordinate Time minus Ship Proper Time at a point in a Journey Timeline.
_Avoid_: Time gained, time lost
